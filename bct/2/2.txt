1. How to Implement the Practical
1. Open the official MetaMask website: metamask.io.
2. Download the MetaMask extension for your browser or the mobile app.
3. Open MetaMask and select Create a new wallet.
4. Create a strong password and continue.
5. MetaMask will show a Secret Recovery Phrase. Write it down and store it securely offline.
6. Confirm the recovery phrase as prompted.
7. Your wallet is created. Open the account menu to view and copy your public wallet address.
8. To receive crypto, share your public address with the sender. To send crypto, select Send, enter the recipient’s address and amount, review the network and transaction fee, then confirm.
Security: Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase or private keys. Anyone who has them can control your wallet. Use only the official MetaMask site or app.

2. Practical Write-up
Aim
To create a cryptocurrency wallet using MetaMask and 
understand how it can be used for crypto transactions.
Theory
MetaMask is a cryptocurrency wallet that allows
 users to manage blockchain accounts and interact 
 with decentralized applications. It provides a 
 public wallet address for receiving cryptocurrency 
 and uses private keys to authorize transactions.

A wallet’s public address can be shared to receive 
funds. Its private key and Secret Recovery Phrase 
must remain confidential because they provide control 
over the wallet. Transactions are recorded on the
 blockchain and may require a network transaction fee,
  commonly called a gas fee.
Conclusion
A MetaMask wallet was created successfully.
 The wallet’s public address can be used to 
 receive cryptocurrency, and the wallet can 
 send crypto by confirming the recipient address, amount, network, and transaction fee.
